Put your certificate images (jpg/png) or PDFs in this folder.
Then open js/script.js, find "certificates:" and add a line, for example:
  {t:'Digital Marketing Course', org:'Issued by', year:'2025', img:'certificates/digital-marketing.jpg'},
For a PDF use  pdf:'certificates/name.pdf'  instead of img.
Delete the three SAMPLE lines when you add your own.
